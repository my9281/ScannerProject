Updated: 2026-11-09.

## 1. Open the right procedure

1. Web: sign in, open **我的账户 → 操作文档与 SOP** (my account → operating documents and SOPs). Checklist tablet: **工作台 → 操作文档** (workspace → operating documents). The account needs `checklist.read`.
2. Choose [中文](../sop/README.md), [English](README.md) or [Español](../sop-es/README.md), then your task. The Web sidebar also groups SOPs by language.
3. The 中文 / English / Español links at the top of each SOP open **the same procedure** in another language. “Back to SOP index” returns to the current language's task list.
4. On tablets, follow the document links to change language. Refreshing the directory selects the default Chinese SOP index again; choose your language afterwards.

## 2. Document language and application language

Changing documents does not change the application's controls. AndroidTester PDA offers English / Español on its home page; English is the default and the choice is remembered. Translations retain Chinese labels from Web, WPF, Checklist and after-sales alongside explanations. This does not mean every application offers a language setting.

## 3. Screenshots, attachments and examples

All languages share screenshots and Excel files from the Chinese SOP folder. Links open the same resources; Chinese text inside images is not automatically translated. Explanations next to Chinese control names help locate them. Pictured dates, SKUs, quantities, charges and customer data are examples. Use complete current work-order data, actual dates/quantities and original working photos; never upload a redacted documentation image as evidence.

The online reader uses Markdown. Existing receiving Word files are separate artifacts; this translation update maintains Markdown. Word downloads are not offered by the reader. Excel downloads use protected links; on Checklist, save/open through the sharing menu. Source-code and unpublished-file links are for repository readers only.

## 4. Check workflow status before acting

- Receiving, good-stock operations, putaway and return-for-repair have substantive text. Good-stock operations still await on-site acceptance; putaway ends at **delivery awaiting confirmation**, not formal completion.
- Solar-panel testing, scrapping, month-end and annual settlement have placeholder Chinese sources. Their translations remain pending until rules are supplied.
- “Documented” means content exists, not on-site approval or acceptance. Translation does not grant permissions or turn unresolved items into approved rules.
- Good-stock pallet reassignment, moves of already put-away stock, PDA upload, work-order completion and formal putaway are different actions. Do not substitute one for another. An OK-only label is not proof of an inspection pass.

## 5. Discrepancies and access problems

Compare ambiguity with the linked Chinese source and ask the responsible person. Pause affected steps if SN, SKU, handling decisions, dates, units or submission actions are uncertain; record the section and discrepancy rather than guessing. When the source changes, maintain both translations, all three index statuses and links together.

If a document will not open, check sign-in and permission, refresh the directory and retry. New Markdown/images/attachments require republishing Scanner.Web's `Documentation/` resources; committing files does not update the running service. See [document access and publishing — Chinese](../文档浏览与发布.md).
